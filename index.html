About

Platform engineering leadership, in practice

I've spent my career zigging and zagging through Software, Data, Reliability, and Platform Engineering. Over the past 18 years I've moved from writing software to building the teams that write it — hiring, mentoring, and setting the technical direction that lets good engineers do their best work.

Today I'm the Sr. Director of Platform Engineering at Twilio, where I lead internal platform across Cloud Foundations, Developer Productivity, Reliability Engineering, and Observability. I care most about the unglamorous parts of leadership: clear expectations, healthy feedback, and shipping simple and elegant software that makes a difference.
