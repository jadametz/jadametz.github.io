I'm an engineering leader focused on data, reliability, and infrastructure engineering. I build teams, design systems, and create cultures where engineers do their best work.

## What I Do

Currently, I'm Director of Engineering for the Developer Platform at Twilio Segment, where I lead teams building the infrastructure that powers customer data platforms at scale. My work focuses on:

- **Platform Engineering**: Designing developer-first platforms that emphasize reliability, performance, and great experiences
- **Team Building**: Growing diverse, high-performing teams and creating organizational structures that enable autonomy and impact
- **Infrastructure at Scale**: Building resilient data systems that handle billions of events and maintain strict SLAs

## Career Journey

### Twilio Segment — Director of Engineering, Developer Platform
Building platforms and teams for one of the leading customer data platforms. Focused on developer experience, reliability, and scalable infrastructure.

### Invoca — Site Reliability Engineer → Engineering Manager
Started as an SRE working on conversation intelligence systems. Transitioned into leadership, where I learned the fundamentals of building and growing engineering teams.

### Graphiq (acquired by Amazon, 2017) — Data Engineering
Pioneered the Data Engineering discipline. Built data ingestion pipelines that handled 25 billion curated relationships. This work became part of Amazon Alexa following the acquisition.

## What I Value

**Specificity over buzzwords**: I care about real outcomes. Not "DevOps culture" in the abstract, but actual deployment frequency, lead time, and mean time to recovery.

**Sustainable engineering**: High performance doesn't mean burnout. The best teams ship reliably because they have processes, ownership, and sustainable on-call practices.

**Continuous improvement**: Every incident is a learning opportunity. Every sprint retrospective matters. Small, compounding improvements beat grand transformations.

**Diverse perspectives**: The best solutions come from teams with different backgrounds, experiences, and ways of thinking.
